## Projects

### Site Suitability Analysis – Airport Relocation (Kwabre District, Ghana)
**Tools used:** ArcGIS  
**Date:** May 2026

Conducted a multi-criteria site suitability analysis to identify suitable locations for relocating 
Prempeh II International Airport within the Kwabre, Effiduase-Asokore, and Kwabre East Districts 
of the Ashanti Region, Ghana.

Suitability criteria included proximity to town centers, distance from highways, elevation slope, 
distance from rivers, and exclusion of forest reserves and existing settlements.

### Multi-Factor Flood Risk Assessment – Greater Accra Region
**Tools used:** ArcGIS  
**Date:** May 2026

Conducted a multi-factor flood risk assessment of the Greater Accra Region 
using three spatial factors: elevation, slope, and proximity to rivers. 
Each factor was mapped and classified independently before being combined 
into a final risk classification identifying high, moderate, and low risk 
zones across the region.

This analysis demonstrates how GIS can support urban planning, early warning 
systems, and disaster preparedness decisions at the local level.
